# Project ownership and career presentation

Public title: **Crypto Agent — Auditable AI Research & Paper-Trading System**.

## Accurate contribution statement

I initiated and led this project based on my own market research needs. My primary contributions include requirements definition, research methodology, feature prioritization, experiment design, iterative evaluation, and acceptance testing. Much of the engineering implementation was developed with AI coding tools such as Codex.

The work involved repeated decisions and evaluations, not a single prompt. It should not be described as independently hand-written code, proof of mastery of every implementation detail, or a fully autonomous trading agent.

## Evidence that can be demonstrated

- A small installable CLI extracted from existing research infrastructure, with explicit public/private separation.
- Causal evidence packets, immutable decision imports, reference/risk admission and hash-linked recovery.
- Conservative paper execution, mandatory/observation event separation, protective orders and failure-path tests.
- A reproducible offline demonstration with clearly identified synthetic prices and mock answers.

A suitable CV summary is:

> Led the requirements, research methodology, experiment design and acceptance evaluation of an auditable AI-assisted market-research project. Used AI coding tools to develop and validate a reproducible offline CLI demonstrating causal evidence, frozen decisions, risk gates and deterministic paper execution.
